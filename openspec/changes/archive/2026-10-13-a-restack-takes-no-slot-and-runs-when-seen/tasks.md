## 1. [agent-build-kit] [tier1] A restack runs in its own lane, takes no slot, and starts when it is seen

Needs: a-conflict-is-resolved-in-one-session-without-holding-the-repo-lock group 2 — the one-session step the lane runs, and the per-phase turn it keeps

Done when a base that moves starts its restack at once, with every build slot busy and the limit on units in progress reached; the restacks run in a lane of at most `limits.max_concurrent_restacks` (default 2, checked at load) beside the build workers, in the order they were seen, one per branch, never under a running step and never while the unit waits on another unit; a unit owed a restack is not started in a worker for it; the merged unit's branch is kept until its children have moved; and no count of units in progress, blocked or queued, no wait reason and no free-worker figure changes because of it. Estimate: about 540 changed lines.

- [x] 1.1 Test: in `tests/test_config_restack_lane.py`, `limits.max_concurrent_restacks` defaults to 2, accepts 1 and 8, and a 0, a negative number, a float and a string are refused when the configuration is loaded, naming the setting.
- [x] 1.2 Test: in `tests/pipeline/test_restack_lane.py`, with a cap of 2 and five restacks handed over together, two start before the hand-over returns, three wait, and each starts in the order seen as one finishes; a unit named twice is queued once and moves onto the base it has when its move starts; a restack that finds its branch claimed (a run holds it) leaves the unit as it was and is moved after the holder's run ends; two restacks of one branch never overlap.
- [x] 1.3 Test: in `tests/pipeline/test_restack_lane_handlers.py`, a merge seen by the poller with every build slot taken starts the child's restack at once and stops no build; a child whose resolver is blocked on an event does not hold up the poll of the rest of the repository's events or the pass's refresh; the merged unit's worktree and branch are kept until the last child that named it has moved and removed after, by the store's and the host's reading; a child with a thread is told its base moved and is handed to the lane; a restack that finds a rewritten branch still moves off the merged base.

## 2. [agent-build-kit] [tier1] The lane honours the usage guard, moves a parked thread, and shows itself

Needs: every-agent-call-is-recorded group 1 — the restack ledger node the lane's resolver calls are recorded under

Done when the lane's resolver is admitted by the same guard, thresholds and estimate as a unit's agent step while a clean move is not asked, a conflicting restack waits for a pause and is moved when it ends, an in-review unit told its base moved is moved by the lane through `prepare` and then started in a worker for what follows, a run's second hold is followed by the lane's move, `abk status` shows `restacks: N running, M waiting` and the unit as restacking, and the documents, the skills and the changelog say so. Estimate: about 520 changed lines.

- [x] 2.1 Test: in `tests/pipeline/test_restack_lane_usage.py`, under a usage pause a clean restack is moved and the guard is not asked, and a conflicting one is aborted inside the turn, left planned with a deferred restack and the branch at its old head; the guard is asked once per resolver call by `build_may_start`'s decision, and with a refused read and room on the estimate the resolver runs on the estimate's say, attributed to the unit being moved; a runtime's own refusal does the same; when the pause ends the next scan moves the unit, asking the guard again; no timer retries a deferred restack before then.
- [x] 2.2 Test: in `tests/graph/test_restack_lane_prepare.py`, a unit `running` with `base_changed` and a thread parked at `prepare` is resumed by the lane through `prepare` and left positioned at the node after it, and `resumable_units` then offers it for what follows; a resolved move writes the predecessor note and clears the approval as `prepare` does in a worker; a clean move of an approved head keeps the approval; a conflict the first two steps cannot settle leaves the unit `planned` with `restack_conflict` for its run's port.
