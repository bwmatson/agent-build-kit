# usage-is-estimated-from-recorded-cost

On 2026-10-21 the usage guard estimated the session window at 95% when it was at 69%: the reading was 54 minutes old, two units had spent since it, and each was counted as the flat 17 points (the 0.9 quantile of points gained per unit over the newest 30 pairs of readings), although only one of them was running a review. The flat figure ignores what a unit did, and spend that belongs to no unit (`plan`, tracks, chat, init) is not counted at all. With the review tiers a unit's cost will vary much more. Here the estimate becomes the recorded cost of the calls since the reading, times a points-per-dollar figure learned from the installation's own pairs of readings (with a small per-hour intercept for use abk does not record, and a residual-quantile safety scale), for every ledger row with or without a unit. It is adopted per window only where a walk-forward comparison shows it no worse than the flat figure on false allows; otherwise, with too few pairs with cost, or with calibration off, today's flat per-unit estimate stands exactly. `abk status` and `abk usage replay` show both, and the stored allowances file gets a versioned shape. The forecast of what a unit about to start will spend, by review tier and by passes per role, is the separate change `a-unit-start-forecasts-its-usage-by-tier`, which reuses the figures made here.
