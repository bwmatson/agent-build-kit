# a-split-moves-the-units-of-archived-changes

`abk planning split` selects unit records only by the change directories that are still active, so the unit records of archived changes (the merged history of the moving repo) stay in the source while their archive entries move, and the dependency check then refuses every moved unit that depends on one of them. This change makes a unit move when every change it builds is a moved active change or a moved archive entry, gives a unit whose change has no directory at all an explicit rule (it moves by its repo, and the plan says so), carries a dependency on a merged unit of another repo as satisfied history with a printed note instead of refusing, splits the plan's unit counts by origin, and keeps `--remove` removing exactly what moved. Priority 0: until it lands, a real copy gives the destination the archive and the specs but none of the history, and the cutover check ("the same units as before; nothing replans") fails.
