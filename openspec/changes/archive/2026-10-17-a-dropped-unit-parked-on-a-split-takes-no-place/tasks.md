## 1. [agent-build-kit] [unit-test] A unit parked on a split holds no place whatever its cause field says, and a replan keeps the park

Done when a `planned` or `unplanned` unit with non-empty `parked_splits` is not in progress whatever work it has and whether or not its cause is set (unless its latest cause is another than `split_held`), so `abk status` leaves it out of the count and the queue-full line and the room to start counts without it, an `unplanned` unit with a pull request or a pushed commit and no parked splits counts as before, a unit whose splits were cleared counts by the normal rules, releasing the split neither resumes nor starts a parked unplanned unit, `abk status` lists planned and unplanned parked units as parked on their split, a replan and a demotion to `unplanned` keep `parked_splits`, `parked_sha`, `parked_snapshot` and `parked_attempts`, and the docs and the `in_progress` docstring say so.
