# Tasks

Acceptance: none — every behaviour here is a term derivation, a configuration merge, a doctor finding, a store rewrite or a generated file, and each is proved by a tier-1 test over temporary directories, fake installations and the command run in process; no consumer drives a surface this change builds beyond what those tests drive.

Priority: 2
