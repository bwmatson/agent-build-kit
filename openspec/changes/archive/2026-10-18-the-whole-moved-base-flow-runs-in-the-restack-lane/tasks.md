# Tasks

Acceptance: none — every behaviour here is a scheduling rule, a count, a stop or a rendered text over a unit's stored record and its thread, and each is proved by a tier-1 test over fake units, a fake graph runner, a fake usage guard and a fake clock; no consumer drives a surface this change builds beyond what those tests drive.

Priority: 2
